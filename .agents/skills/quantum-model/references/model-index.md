# Project model names and aliases

Resolve active implementations first:

- transverse-field-ising (TFIM): `.knowledge/models/transverse-field-ising/MODEL.md`
  routes to random_tfim or clean tfim (both currently OBC).
- rydberg-pxp (PXP): `.knowledge/models/rydberg-pxp/MODEL.md`
  distinguishes current pxp_model from the historical staggered deformation.
- random-bond classical Ising (RBIM): `.knowledge/projects/random_ising.md`.

Background cards retained for comparison: `anderson-localization`,
`mbl-disordered-heisenberg`, and the tensor-network benchmark `aklt`.
Their cards live at `.knowledge/models/<name>/MODEL.md`; they are not active subprojects.
Tensor-network and quantum-circuit methods: `.knowledge/methods/INDEX.md`.
Independent exact-state/circuit benchmarks: `.knowledge/solvable/INDEX.md`.
For other models, do not assume the old generic catalog still exists.
