Figure 4: L=[10, 24, 40, 50], T=[0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1.02, 1.5, 2.0, 2.8]; absolute value of the stored disorder-averaged fixed-origin spin autocorrelation on log-log axes.
Absolute value is taken after disorder averaging. t=0 is omitted; exactly zero means are plotted as gaps. Shading maps the original mean ± stored SEM interval through absolute value; intervals touching or crossing zero are omitted, with no artificial positive floor.
C(t)=sum_i s_i(t0+t)*s_i(t0)/L^2; reference state is the final SW configuration, followed by 4096 heat-bath sweeps. No connected subtraction or time-origin averaging.
T=2.80 is included to cover the high-temperature paramagnetic regime; the remaining selected temperatures retain run_004 comparison points. T=1.02 is not assumed to be the run_005 heat-capacity maximum.
A heat-capacity maximum is not asserted to be a critical temperature.
Figure 5: L=50, T=[1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 1.6]; y=ln(C) versus x=(ln(t))^2, using the signed disorder mean without absolute value. Both transformed axes are linear. Fit: ln(C)=intercept+slope*(ln(t))^2.
Plot and unweighted OLS fit use consecutive times from t=2, stopping before the first failure of 0<C<1 and C>3 SEM. Dashed lines: y=intercept+slope*x. Parameters, R², point counts and time windows: 05_autocorrelation_fits.csv.
Fits are descriptive and window-dependent. Adjacent times are correlated; R² does not establish an asymptotic decay law, and independent-point parameter errors are not reported.
